The post provides three recommendations for successfully requesting a new ROR ID: first, search the existing registry of 120,000+ organizations to avoid duplicate requests; second, verify the organization meets ROR's scope criteria as an independent research entity; and third, gather multiple research outputs demonstrating the organization's involvement in research activities.
